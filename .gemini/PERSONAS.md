# Alete-Team: Persona Registry (Edge-Pulse)

This document defines the core personas for the `edge-pulse` library. All product decisions, features, and documentation must be mapped to the specific survival needs and cognitive advantages of these personas.

---

## 1. The Neural Architect (The Substrate Specialist)

**Name:** Leo
**Role:** Data/Edge Infrastructure Engineer
**Core Lens:** Structural Resilience & System Thermodynamics

### Profile
Leo is an elite engineer at a high-growth AI startup. He views centralized data ingestion as "Architectural Entropy"—it's slow, expensive, and a single point of failure. He is obsessed with pushing compute to the "Edge" to minimize latency and maximize system metabolic efficiency.

### Objectives
- Build a "Nervous System" for web data that filters noise at the source.
- Reduce centralized LLM token costs by pre-processing and bucketing data before it hits the core.
- Ensure the ingestion substrate can handle sudden bursts of web traffic without failure.

### Survival Pains
- **Technical Entropy:** Heavy HTML parsing libraries that crash edge runtimes (Cloudflare Workers, Vercel Edge).
- **Metabolic Friction:** High egress costs and slow ingestion pipelines that prevent real-time "alive" features.
- **Signal Decay:** Data that is stale by the time it reaches the user.

### Edge-Pulse TTV (Time To Value)
- **Immediate:** Drop-in edge-compatible HTML extraction.
- **Strategic:** NLP bucketing that turns raw chaos into structured "Cognitive Augments" at the ingestion layer.

## 2. The Feedback Loop Strategist (The Optimizer)

**Name:** Sarah
**Role:** Senior AI Product Manager
**Core Lens:** Metabolic Efficiency & Adaptive Fitness

### Profile
Sarah is "The Optimizer". She manages a market intelligence platform that promises users a "Performance Edge". She needs her product to feel responsive, intelligent, and "Neural"—as if it's anticipating the market's pulse.

### Objectives
- Achieve the fastest "Signal-to-Insight" loop in the industry.
- Maximize ROI (Metabolic Efficiency) by reducing the token-cost-per-insight.
- Create a "Neural Upgrade" for her product's data ingestion layer.

### Survival Pains
- **Cognitive Noise:** Irrelevant web data cluttering the user's focus and reducing "Cognitive Sovereignty".
- **Economic Pressure:** High operational costs from centralized parsing cutting into product margins.
- **Fitness Gap:** Competitors releasing features faster because their data substrate is more flexible.

### Edge-Pulse TTV (Time To Value)
- **Immediate:** Real-time data enrichment from any URL.
- **Strategic:** Ability to launch niche "Signal Feeds" based on NLP buckets that were previously too expensive to parse.

## 3. The Signal Dominance Lead (The Alpha-Curator)

**Name:** Marcus
**Role:** GTM / Growth Strategy
**Core Lens:** Cognitive Sovereignty & Narrative Archery

### Profile
Marcus is "The Alpha-Curator". He is responsible for the product's external "Plumage" and market positioning. He sells the vision of a "Neural Upgrade"—a tool that gives users a "Deep Understanding" of reality that others lack.

### Objectives
- Position the product as a "High-Status" necessity for anyone needing a competitive edge.
- Differentiate from "Generic LLM Wrappers" by highlighting unique, edge-processed data streams.
- Claim "Signal Supremacy" through near-instant ingestion of global data.

### Survival Pains
- **Narrative Entropy:** Being seen as just another tool in a noisy market.
- **Competitive Signaling:** Faster, cheaper rivals stealing the "Performance Edge" narrative.
- **Perspective Loss:** Lacking the technical "Signal" to back up bold marketing claims.

### Edge-Pulse TTV (Time To Value)
- **Immediate:** Blazing-fast performance benchmarks to use in marketing.
- **Strategic:** Exclusive access to "Edge-Enriched" datasets that competitors cannot replicate at scale.
